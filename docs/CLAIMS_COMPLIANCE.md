# Nutritional & Health Claims Compliance Register

This document records the distinction between **published product statements** on the EastField India website and **client-supplied traditional/marketing claims withheld pending scientific and regulatory substantiation**.

## 1. Published Poster Statements (Public Website)

The public **WHY MAKHANA?** section presents responsible, non-medical statements framed around a balanced diet and plain makhana's natural nutritional profile:

- `SUPPORTS HEART HEALTH` (naturally low in fat in plain form; magnesium & potassium)
- `SUPPORTS DIGESTIVE HEALTH` (dietary fibre)
- `RICH IN ANTIOXIDANTS` (naturally occurring polyphenols & flavonoids; research ongoing)
- `SUPPORTS BONE HEALTH` (naturally occurring calcium, magnesium & phosphorus)
- `SOURCE OF PLANT-BASED NUTRIENTS` (botanical *Euryale ferox* seed kernel)
- `SUPPORTS WEIGHT MANAGEMENT` (light, low-fat plain popped snack)
- `A SATISFYING, CRUNCHY SNACK`
- `NUTRIENT-RICH`
- `TRADITIONAL INDIAN SUPERFOOD`
- `GUILT-FREE. GLUTEN-FREE.` (accurate to plain raw makhana; finished consumer product gluten-free status depends on agreed processing and packing controls)

## 2. Client-Provided Claims Held Pending Substantiation

The following traditional/marketing phrases supplied by the client are **intentionally excluded** from public website claims unless and until authoritative, regulator-compliant substantiation is provided for the target market (FSSAI / FDA / EFSA):

1. Good for diabetic patients
2. Maintains kidney health
3. Detoxifies liver
4. Improves bone strength
5. Cure for diarrhea
6. Helps build muscle mass
7. Good for fertility in men
8. Prevents skin problems
9. Anti-aging benefits
